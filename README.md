### Artifact 2: Machine Learning vs. Deep Learning in Customer Analytics

**Project Type:** Individual Research Report  
**Focus:** Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Business Applications

This project compares traditional machine learning and deep learning through two real-world business applications: customer churn prediction in the telecommunications industry and natural language processing for consumer feedback analysis.

The report examines why different AI approaches are appropriate for different types of data and business problems. It also connects these applications to my background in market research and consumer analytics.

**Skills Demonstrated:**
- Machine learning and deep learning fundamentals
- Customer analytics
- Natural language processing
- Research and critical thinking
- Applying AI concepts to business problems

**View Project:** [Machine Learning vs. Deep Learning Report](artifacts/02-machine-learning-vs-deep-learning/ML-vs-DL-Customer-Analytics.pdf)
