# Machine Learning vs. Deep Learning in Customer Analytics

## Project Overview

This individual project compares traditional machine learning and deep learning through two customer analytics applications: customer churn prediction and consumer feedback analysis.

The project explores how different AI approaches can support business decision-making and why the choice of method depends on the type of data and the problem being addressed.

## Project Objectives

- Explain the differences between traditional machine learning and deep learning
- Explore real-world applications of both approaches
- Evaluate why different methods are suitable for different analytical problems
- Connect AI techniques to practical customer analytics applications

## Methods Explored

### Traditional Machine Learning: Support Vector Machine (SVM)

SVM is explored as an approach for predicting customer churn using structured customer data, such as service usage, contract information, and customer characteristics.

### Deep Learning: Transformer-Based NLP

Transformer-based models are explored for analyzing unstructured consumer feedback, including customer reviews and open-ended survey responses.

## Professional Relevance

My previous experience in market research and consumer analytics involved understanding customer behavior, interpreting survey findings, and translating data into business recommendations.

This project connects that experience with AI applications. For example, machine learning can help organizations identify customers at risk of leaving, while deep learning can help analyze large volumes of consumer feedback and uncover patterns in customer opinions.

## Key Learning Outcomes

This project strengthened my understanding of how machine learning and deep learning differ in their applications. It also helped me consider how AI techniques can complement traditional market research and support more informed business decisions.
