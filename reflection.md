# Portfolio Development Reflection

## Second Portfolio Iteration

For the second iteration of my professional portfolio, I focused on improving its organization, accessibility, and presentation. In my initial submission, I had uploaded my AI development timeline presentation, but the portfolio needed more structure and context to make the work easier to understand and evaluate.

I reorganized the repository by creating a portfolio homepage, adding project overview pages, and organizing my academic work into separate sections. I also added a second project, Machine Learning vs. Deep Learning in Customer Analytics, to demonstrate a different set of skills.

The AI Development Timeline project demonstrates my understanding of the historical development of artificial intelligence, while the Machine Learning vs. Deep Learning project highlights my ability to research, compare, and evaluate AI approaches in practical business applications.

Through these improvements, I learned that a professional portfolio should do more than display completed assignments. It should also explain the purpose of each project, the skills demonstrated, and how the work connects to professional goals.

## Future Improvements

I plan to continue expanding my portfolio with additional projects involving Python, data analytics, machine learning, and real-world business applications. I also want to improve the visual presentation of the portfolio as I develop more technical skills.
